# PPT Content – CareerAI

## Slide 1 – Title
CareerAI
AI-Powered Career Guidance & Job Matching System
SDG 8 – Decent Work and Economic Growth

## Slide 2 – Problem
- Skilled job seekers may not know which roles fit their skills.
- Career choices can be confusing.
- Users need to know which skills to learn next.

## Slide 3 – Proposed Solution
CareerAI collects skills, interests and preferred location and recommends suitable career paths with match scores and skill gaps.

## Slide 4 – Target Users
Students, fresh graduates, entry-level job seekers and career switchers.

## Slide 5 – Features
Skill profile, interest profile, location preference, career matching, match score, skill-gap analysis.

## Slide 6 – System Flow
User Input → API → Skill/Location/Interest Matching → Ranking → Career Recommendation → Skill Gap

## Slide 7 – Technology
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python Flask, local role dataset.

Resume parser, live jobs, ML recommendations, course recommendations, multilingual support.

CareerAI helps users move from “What job suits me?” to a clear career direction and learning plan.
